# Atlassian Cloud Migration: User Communication Plan

A simple template for keeping users informed during a move to Atlassian Cloud. Most migration questions from users are about timing and what will change, so clear messages at the right moments help.

## Four moments to communicate

### 4 weeks before
- What is happening and why
- The planned date and how long access may be limited
- Who to contact with questions

### 1 week before
- A reminder of the date and time
- What users should finish or save before the move
- What will look different afterwards, such as the site address and sign-in steps

### On the day
- A message when work starts and a message when it ends
- Where to report problems
- Any known limits in the first hours

### After the move
- A short guide to what changed
- Where to find help and training
- A request for feedback, and a promise to review it

## Tips

- Use plain words. Avoid internal project names.
- Send messages from a person users know.
- Keep one page that always shows the latest status.
- Ask a small group to read the messages first.
